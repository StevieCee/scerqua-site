Why Coral Ridge Isles?

Coral Ridge Isles remains one of Fort Lauderdale's most desirable residential communities. Buyers are drawn to the neighborhood's oversized lots, mature landscaping, proximity to the beach, and convenient access to major employment centers throughout Broward County.

Renovated homes in the neighborhood continue to attract strong interest due to limited inventory, ongoing investment in surrounding areas, and the growing demand for move-in-ready properties with modern amenities.

For buyers seeking a home that combines lifestyle, location, and significant capital improvements, 1718 NE 58th Street represents a rare opportunity to enjoy the benefits of extensive upgrades without the disruption and cost of completing them yourself.
