About Steve Cerqua

Three decades of leadership, vision, and strategic growth across technology and global ventures

Professional Foundation

I am a technology executive at Galaxy with a proven track record of building high-performing organizations that deliver transformative results. Over more than three decades, I have led quality engineering organizations, architected software delivery programs, pioneered automation initiatives, and guided technology transformation at scale across complex, regulated industries.

My work in fintech combines rigorous engineering discipline with the speed and accountability required in financial technology. I have successfully scaled teams from startup environments to enterprise-level operations, consistently delivering measurable business outcomes while building cultures of innovation and accountability.

Key areas of expertise include:

  • Quality Engineering Leadership & Organizational Design
  • Fintech Software Delivery Transformation & Automation Programs
  • Engineering Team Building & Talent Development
  • Cross-functional Collaboration & Change Management
  • Technology Strategy & Digital Transformation

Beyond the Boardroom

While technology leadership defines much of my professional identity, I am also a licensed real estate agent associated with Santa Stella Real Estate. I bring the same care for detail and clear communication to real estate opportunities that I apply to my technology work.

This multifaceted approach to personal and professional development has led me to:

  • Develop substantial real estate portfolios across three countries
  • Build strategic business relationships across multiple continents
  • Pursue Italian citizenship through ancestral lineage
  • Create a lifestyle centered on meaningful international living
  • Document and share insights from these diverse experiences

This website is a reflection of these interconnected pursuits—a space where I share professional insights, investment perspectives, and the ongoing journey of building an intentional global life.
